3. How buying works

  • Buyers pay upfront from their SplitSub wallet. You can top up the wallet with Paystack (cards, bank transfer and other methods Paystack offers).
  • SplitSub sets the price and the number of seats for each plan. The price you see before you confirm is the price you pay.
  • After you pay, the host sends or gives you access. Follow the host's note and use only your own seat.
  • If something is wrong, use the Report option on the order so our team can help (see the Refund & Cancellation Policy).

4. How hosting works

  • You may list only a plan you really hold and are allowed to share. We may ask for proof, and listings are reviewed before they go live.
  • You must give each buyer the access you promised, for the full period, and reply to reports.
  • Your earnings are held in escrow for 30 days after each purchase. After that they move to your available balance and you can withdraw them.
  • If a buyer is refunded, the matching earning is taken back.
