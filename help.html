Frequently asked questions

What is SplitSub?

A marketplace where a host lists a plan they pay for and buyers pay for a seat in it, so everyone pays less. SplitSub holds the money safely until the host has delivered.

Is my money safe?

Yes. When you buy a seat, your payment is held by SplitSub (escrow). The host is paid after the escrow period, and only if the order is in good standing. If something goes wrong, you can open a dispute from your order.

How do I add money to my wallet?

Open Wallet and tap Top up. You pay with Paystack (card, bank transfer and other methods Paystack offers). The minimum deposit is ₦100. The money shows in your wallet after Paystack confirms it.

My top-up shows pending or did not arrive

Wait a few minutes and reopen the app. If your bank has debited you and the wallet is still not updated, message support with your Paystack reference and we will check it.

What does it cost to buy?

The seat price plus a flat ₦200 fee per purchase. The price is set by SplitSub, so hosts cannot change it on the day.

How do I become a host?

Go to Host, pick the service and plan you really hold and are allowed to share, and submit it. We may ask for proof, and we review each listing before it goes live. You agree to the escrow terms when you list.

When do hosts get paid, and how do I withdraw?

Earnings stay pending for the escrow period (30 days), then move to your withdrawable earnings. Request a withdrawal from Earnings (minimum ₦1,000). The fee is shown before you confirm and depends on the amount. Our team pays it to a Nigerian bank account in your name.

Can I cancel or get a refund?

Yes, within the rules in our Refund & Cancellation Policy. A cancellation fee applies and the ₦200 purchase fee is not refunded. Refunds go back to your SplitSub wallet.

I paid but I can't get in

Check the order page for the host's instructions, and message the host in the order chat first. If you still have no access, open a dispute from the order or message support. Do not wait until the end of the period.

Which services can I find here?

The services shown on the home page. Can't find yours? Use Subscription requests to tell us what to add.

How do I close my account?

Withdraw your money and finish your open orders, then message support and we will close it.

How payments work

  1. You top up your wallet. Pay with Paystack. We confirm every payment with Paystack on our server before the money reaches your wallet.
  2. You pay for a seat. The price plus the ₦200 fee comes out of your wallet. You pay upfront.
  3. SplitSub holds the money (escrow). The host does not get it yet. This protects you if access is not delivered.
  4. The host delivers access and you confirm or use it. If there is a problem, open a dispute and we step in.
  5. Escrow ends. After 30 days the host's share (minus our 10% commission) becomes withdrawable.
  6. The host withdraws. They request a withdrawal and our team pays it to their bank account, minus the withdrawal fee shown in the Terms.

Every movement of money is recorded in an account history that cannot be edited. You can see yours in your wallet. We never ask for your card details inside SplitSub; card payments happen on Paystack's page.

How access is delivered

How you get in depends on the service and plan. It is shown on the plan before you buy and again on your order page.

  • Invitation: the host invites you to their family or group plan using the email or phone number you gave. Accept the invitation from your email or the app.
  • Shared login or profile: the host gives you the details for your own profile on the plan. Keep them private and do not change the password.
  • Other methods: some plans use a link, a code or a seat the host sets up for you. The order page tells you exactly what to do.

The host's instructions appear on your order page, and you can chat with the host from the same page. Hosts must deliver the access they promised for the full period. If access stops working, tell the host in the order chat, and open a dispute if it is not fixed. Never share your access details with other people.
