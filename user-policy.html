The rules every buyer and host on SplitSub agrees to follow.

SplitSub only works when people are honest. This policy applies to everyone: buyers, hosts and anyone who contacts us.

Do

  • Be truthful about your plan, price, access and proof.
  • Hosts: give working access for the whole period and answer reports quickly.
  • Buyers: use only your own seat, follow the host's note, and report problems through the app.
  • Be polite to hosts, buyers and our team.
  • Keep your account details and 6-digit codes private.

Do not

  • List a plan you do not own or are not allowed to share, or use stolen, hacked or fake accounts, cards or proof.
  • Take buyers or hosts away from SplitSub to deal or pay outside the platform.
  • Change the password or remove a buyer's access during a paid period.
  • Share your seat, resell access, or let other people use your login.
  • Open more than one account, or abuse referral codes or promos.
  • Open a false report, or file a chargeback instead of using our report process.
  • Harass, threaten or insult anyone, or impersonate another person or SplitSub.
  • Send illegal, harmful or adult content, spam or links to scams.
  • Try to break, test or get around the site's security or payment checks.
  • Use SplitSub for money laundering or any other crime.

What happens if rules are broken

Depending on how serious it is, we may: remove a listing; warn you; limit or suspend your account; hold your balance while we investigate; refund affected buyers from the host's earnings; and report serious cases to the police or the service provider.

Report a problem

If you see something wrong, open the order and tap Report, or message us on WhatsApp.
