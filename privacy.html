How SplitSub collects, uses and protects your personal information.

This policy explains what personal information SplitSub collects, why, and your choices. We follow the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA).

1. What we collect

  • Account: your email, username, name and password (the password is stored hashed by our login provider, we cannot read it).
  • Orders and listings: the plans you list or join, the contact you give for access, notes, and proof or screenshots you upload.
  • Money: wallet and payment history, Paystack payment references, and the bank name, account number and account name you give for withdrawals. We never see or store your full card number, Paystack handles that.
  • Messages: reports, replies and support messages.
  • Technical: basic logs such as device type and time of visits, used for security and fixing problems.

2. Why we use it

  • To create your account, confirm your email and sign you in.
  • To run orders, hold money in escrow, pay hosts, process refunds and withdrawals.
  • To send important emails: codes, receipts, order updates, security alerts.
  • To stop fraud and misuse, and to follow the law.
  • To give support and to improve the service.

3. Who sees your information

  • The other person in an order. A buyer and host see what they need to complete the order, such as a username and the contact or access details given for that order.
  • Service providers who help us run SplitSub: Supabase (database and login), Paystack (payments), Resend (email sending), and our website hosting and DNS providers. They only process data for us.
  • Authorities when the law requires it, or to protect people from fraud.

We do not sell your personal data.

4. Where it is kept

Our providers may store data on servers outside Nigeria. We choose providers that protect data and we only share what is needed.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is open. Payment and order records may be kept for as long as the law and fraud checks require, even after the account is closed.

6. Security

Connections are encrypted, access to data is limited by account, and money records cannot be edited after they are written. No system is perfectly safe, so keep your password private and never share your 6-digit codes with anyone, including us.

7. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, to object to how we use it, or to withdraw consent. Message us on WhatsApp or through Help in the app. You can also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

8. Children

SplitSub is for people 18 and older. If we find an account belonging to a child, we will close it.

9. Browser storage

We keep a small sign-in record in your browser so you stay signed in. We do not use advertising trackers.

10. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date at the top when we do.

11. Help

For answers about payments, access and your account, see our Help Centre and contact support.
