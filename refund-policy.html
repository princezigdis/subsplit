Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated 13 October 2026

How refunds and cancellations work on SplitSub.

In short: if the host or SplitSub is at fault, you get your money back. If you simply cancel, a 30% cancellation fee applies to the seat price. The ₦200 purchase fee is not refunded.

1. When you get a full refund

We refund the seat price in full (and normally the ₦200 fee too) when:

  • the host does not give you the access they promised;
  • the access does not work and the host cannot fix it after you report it;
  • the listing was fake, removed or not allowed;
  • you were charged twice or charged by mistake.

To start, open the order and tap Report. Our team looks at both sides and decides on a full refund, a part refund, or no refund, and tells you the result.

2. Cancelling a seat (30% cancellation fee)

You can ask to cancel a seat you paid for. If the reason is not a fault of the host or SplitSub (for example you changed your mind), then:

ItemWhat happens
Cancellation fee30% of the seat price is kept
RefundThe other 70% of the seat price
₦200 purchase feeNot refunded

Example: a seat costs ₦5,000. A cancellation costs ₦1,500 (30%), so ₦3,500 comes back to your SplitSub wallet. The ₦200 fee you paid is not returned.

To cancel, message support in the app (Help → Message support) or on WhatsApp +234 708 500 2123 with your order reference. Please cancel before the period ends. After a period has ended there is nothing left to refund.

3. If a host cancels

If a host stops or removes a listing while buyers still have time left, those buyers are refunded in full for the unused period, and the matching earnings are taken back from the host.

4. Where the money goes and how long it takes

  • Refunds are paid into your SplitSub wallet. From there you can use it on another seat or withdraw it. Withdrawals carry the normal withdrawal fee and the ₦1,000 minimum (see the Terms of Service).
  • We review reports as quickly as we can and tell you the outcome in the app and by email.

5. Wallet top-ups and failed payments

  • Money you add to your wallet is yours to spend or withdraw. It is not refunded back to the card.
  • If Paystack took your money but your wallet was not credited, send us the payment reference on WhatsApp. We will check it and either credit your wallet or return the money.

6. Chargebacks

Please talk to us before asking your bank for a chargeback. A chargeback made while an order is being handled can lead to account suspension.

7. Escrow

Host earnings are held for 30 days. If a buyer is refunded during that time, the matching earning is cancelled.
